Interest Pro – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 5 November 2026

This policy describes how the Android app Interest Pro (package com.hemanttekan.interestpro) handles information.

Information we collect

Interest Pro does not collect, store, share or sell any personal or device information. The app works completely offline and does not request internet access.

How the app works

All calculations (simple and compound interest) happen only on your device. The numbers you type are not saved or sent anywhere, and are cleared when you close the app.

Permissions, ads and third parties

The app asks for no special permissions, shows no advertisements, and contains no analytics or third-party tracking tools.

Children

Because no data is collected, the app is safe for users of all ages and does not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

For any question about this policy, please write to the developer email address shown on the app's Google Play Store page.
